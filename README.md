# Privacy Policy for Afterglow

Effective Date: July 10, 2026

Afterglow is designed to be a private, local-first reading app. Your books, book contents, reading position, and reading history stay on your device unless you choose to move, export, sync, or back them up using features provided by your device or operating system.

## Information We Collect

Afterglow does not require an account and does not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, payment information, advertising identifier, or book contents.

To maintain and improve the app, Afterglow uses Sentry, a third-party error-monitoring service. Sentry may receive limited technical and diagnostic information when the app is used, including:

- Crash reports, app hangs, and other diagnostic information
- Device model, operating-system version, app version, and similar technical details
- Limited feature-interaction and reader-preference events, such as app launches, reader sessions, and appearance settings
- Network information, such as IP address, as handled by Sentry’s service

We use this information only to diagnose crashes, improve reliability and performance, and understand how app features are used. We do not use it for advertising, marketing, or tracking you across apps or websites.

Afterglow does not intentionally send book titles, book contents, or detailed reading history to Sentry.

## Optional Problem Reports

You may choose to send an in-app problem report. If you do, the description you provide is sent to Sentry along with related diagnostic information so we can investigate the issue.

Please do not include sensitive personal information in a problem report.

## Book Files and Reading Data

Books, reading progress, highlights, and app preferences are stored locally on your device. Reading preferences, such as themes, typography, margins, and appearance settings, are primarily used locally to provide the app’s functionality.

If you use iCloud backup, device backup, file sharing, or another system-provided sync or export feature, Apple’s or the relevant provider’s privacy practices apply to that activity.

## Analytics and Tracking

Afterglow does not use advertising networks or sell, rent, or share personal information for advertising purposes.

We do not track you across apps or websites, and we do not use collected data for targeted advertising.

## Third-Party Services

Afterglow uses Sentry for crash reporting, diagnostics, and limited product analytics. Sentry processes this information on our behalf to help us maintain and improve Afterglow. You can learn more in [Sentry’s Privacy Policy](https://sentry.io/privacy/).

## Children’s Privacy

Afterglow is not designed to collect personal information from children or any other users. If a user voluntarily includes personal information in an optional problem report, it may be processed as described above.

## Your Choices

You can avoid sending an optional problem-report description by not submitting a report. For questions about privacy or to request help with data related to a submitted report, contact us at the address below.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when Afterglow’s features or data practices change. We will update the effective date when we do.
