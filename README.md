# Privacy Policy for Afterglow

Effective Date: May 15, 2026

Afterglow is designed to be a private reading app. We do not collect, store, sell, rent, or share personal information.

## Information We Collect

Afterglow does not collect personal information from users.

We do not collect:

- Names
- Email addresses
- Phone numbers
- Location data
- Contacts
- Photos
- Browsing history
- Reading history
- Book contents
- Analytics data
- Advertising identifiers
- Payment information

## Book Files and Reading Data

Any books you add to Afterglow remain on your device unless you choose to move, export, sync, or back them up using features provided by your device or operating system.

Afterglow may store app preferences locally on your device, such as reading appearance settings, theme choices, typography settings, or other reading preferences. These settings are used only to provide the app’s functionality and are not transmitted to us.

## Analytics and Tracking

Afterglow does not use third-party analytics tools, advertising networks, or tracking technologies.

We do not track you across apps or websites.

## Third-Party Services

Afterglow does not share user data with third-party services.

If you use Apple system features such as iCloud backup, device backup, or file sharing, Apple’s privacy policy and settings may apply to that activity.

## Children’s Privacy

Afterglow does not knowingly collect personal information from children or from any other users.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if Afterglow’s features or data practices change. If we make changes, we will update the effective date above.
